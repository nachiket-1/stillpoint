A small antidote to the scroll.

Most of the internet is built to keep you moving. Tap, swipe, refresh. Stillpoint is built to keep you still.

It began with a question: what if a website could feel like the moment you step out onto a porch at dusk — that first long breath, the world a little quieter than the one inside? So I built six worlds — a dawn chorus, a hearth, a tideline, a nightwood, a soft rain, and a brook — and a small timer to put a frame around your work.

No accounts. No analytics breathing down your neck. No dark patterns. Open it, listen, leave. Or stay a while.
